DecoNova privacy

DecoNova is a hackathon project that redesigns a photo of your room. This page explains what happens to your data. Last updated 30 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name from Google sign-in, used only to identify your account.
  • The room photos you choose to upload or take, and the designs made from them.
  • A count of your remaining credits.

Who processes it

  • Your account, rooms and credits are stored with Supabase.
  • When you tap Make it over, or read a room, your photo is sent to an AI provider (Anthropic for text and room understanding, Google Gemini for images) to produce the result. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • The site is hosted on Netlify.

Sharing

A photo is visible to other people only if you press Share for a family vote. The link works for 30 days and you can stop sharing at any time.

Delete your data

Email prashant.southavenue@gmail.com from your sign-in address and we will delete your account, photos and designs.
